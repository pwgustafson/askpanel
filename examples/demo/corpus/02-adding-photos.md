# Adding photos

You can add photos from your computer, your phone, or another Orchard album.

**From a computer.** Open **Albums**, pick an album (or make one first, see "Making and
organising albums"), and choose **Add photos**. Pick the files, or drag them onto the
album. Orchard accepts JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and short videos up to 2 minutes. Each file can be
up to 200 MB.

**From a phone.** Open the album and tap **Add photos**. Orchard asks for permission to
see your camera roll the first time. Pick as many photos as you like and tap **Add**. You
can keep using Orchard while they finish; a small progress ring shows at the top of the
album until every photo is in.

**What happens after you add a photo.** Orchard keeps the original. It reads the date the
photo was taken and uses that to place it in the album, most recent at the top. If a
photo has no date (a scan, for example), it uses the day you added it; you can change a
photo's date from its **Details** panel. Orchard also looks for faces so you can tag the
people in it (see "Tagging people").

**If a photo does not appear.** Give it a minute; very large videos take longer. If the
progress ring finished and the photo is still missing, the file may be a kind Orchard
does not accept. Try again with a JPEG or PNG. If it still does not appear, send the
album name and the photo's file name to the team.

**Duplicates.** If you add the same photo twice, Orchard keeps only one and tells you.
