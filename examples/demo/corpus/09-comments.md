# Comments

Anyone who can see a photo can comment on it.

**Adding a comment.** Open the photo and type in the box under it. Press Enter to post.
Comments are plain text; you can add emoji but not photos or links that turn into
previews.

**Who sees comments.** Everyone the album is shared with. Comments are not visible
outside the Family.

**Editing or removing a comment.** Tap your own comment and choose **Edit** or
**Delete**. The album owner can delete anyone's comment on photos in their album.

**Being told about comments.** You are notified about comments on photos you added, and
on any photo you have already commented on. See "Notifications" to change this.

**Reactions.** Tap the heart under a comment to react. Orchard shows the number of hearts
and, when you tap it, who they came from.
