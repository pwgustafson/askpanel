# Sharing albums

Albums are private to the person who made them until shared. Sharing is always with
people in your Family; there are no public links.

**Sharing with people.** Open the album, choose **Share**, and pick people from your
Family. They see the album under **Albums** with a small "shared" badge, and get a
notification if they have those turned on.

**Sharing with a group.** If you share the same album with the same relatives often,
make a group under **Sharing** (**New group**, name it, pick people). Then pick the group
in the **Share** sheet. Adding someone to a group later gives them every album shared
with that group.

**What shared people can do.** Anyone an album is shared with can look at every photo,
favourite photos, and add photos of their own. They can remove or delete only the photos
they added themselves. They cannot rename the album, change its cover, share it further,
or delete it. Only the album's owner (the person who made it) can do those things.

**Seeing who an album is shared with.** Open the album and choose **Share**; the sheet
lists everyone. Tap a name and choose **Stop sharing** to remove them. They lose access
straight away, including to photos they added, though those photos remain in their own
library.

**Sharing a single photo.** Orchard does not share single photos; make an album with the
photos you want to share.

**People outside Orchard.** Grandparents who do not use Orchard can be invited to the
Family by the Organizer (see "Family and members"). There is no way to send a link that
works without an account.

**"I shared an album but they cannot see it."** Check three things: they are in your
Family (**Sharing** lists everyone); the album shows their name in its **Share** sheet;
and they have opened Orchard since you shared, as the list refreshes when the app opens.
