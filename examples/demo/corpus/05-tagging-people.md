# Tagging people

Orchard finds faces in your photos and groups the ones that look alike so you can put a
name to them once instead of on every photo.

**Naming someone the first time.** Open **People**. Untitled groups of faces appear as
"Who is this?". Choose one, type the person's name, and press Enter. Every photo in that
group is now tagged with that name.

**When Orchard gets it wrong.** If a group mixes two people, open it, choose **Select**,
tick the photos that are not that person, and choose **Not this person**. Orchard moves
them to a new "Who is this?" group. If two groups are the same person, open one, choose
**Merge with…**, and pick the other.

**Tagging by hand.** Open a photo, choose **Tag people**, tap a face, and type a name.
You can also tag someone whose face is not visible (a baby wrapped up, someone from
behind) by choosing **Add person without a face**.

**Removing a tag.** Open the photo, choose **Tag people**, tap the name, and choose
**Remove tag**.

**Who can see tags.** Names are visible to everyone the album is shared with. Face groups
on the **People** screen only include photos you can see.

**Turning face grouping off.** In **Settings**, switch off **Group faces**. Orchard stops
looking for faces in new photos and hides the "Who is this?" groups. Names you already
added stay on the photos.

**Children and consent.** Orchard is a private family space and does not share faces or
names with anyone outside your Family. If a relative asks not to be tagged, remove their
tags and, if you like, ask the Organizer to remove their person entry from **People**
with **Delete person**.
