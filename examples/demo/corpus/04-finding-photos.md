# Finding photos

**Search.** The search box at the top looks through album names, descriptions, the names
of tagged people, and the place a photo was taken (when the photo has one). Type
"grandma beach 2019" and Orchard shows photos tagged Grandma, in albums mentioning the
beach, taken in 2019.

**By person.** Open **People** and choose someone to see every photo they are tagged in,
across all albums you can see.

**By date.** In any album, tap the month name on the right edge to jump to that month.
In search, type a year or a month and year ("July 2021").

**By place.** If a photo was taken with a phone that records location, Orchard shows the
town under the photo in its **Details** panel, and you can search by that town. Orchard
never shows a map or exact location, and place names are only visible to your Family.

**Favourites.** Tap the heart on any photo to add it to **Favourites**, which is your own
list; other people in your Family do not see which photos you favourited.

**What search does not do.** Search does not read the contents of a photo. It cannot find
"dog" or "birthday cake" unless those words are in an album name, a description, or a
person's name. If you want that, send the idea to the team from the help panel.
