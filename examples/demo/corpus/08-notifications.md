# Notifications

Orchard can let you know when something happens in an album you can see.

**What you can be notified about.** Someone shares an album with you; someone adds
photos to an album shared with you; someone tags you in a photo; someone comments on a
photo you added. Each can be turned on or off separately.

**Turning notifications on or off.** Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**. Choose
whether each kind arrives as a push notification on your phone, an email, both, or
neither.

**Daily summary.** If several things happen in one day, Orchard groups them into one
email in the evening instead of sending several. Turn this off under **Notifications** if
you prefer separate emails.

**Quiet hours.** Push notifications are held between 10 pm and 7 am in your time zone and
delivered in the morning. You can change the hours under **Notifications**.

**Not receiving anything.** On a phone, check that Orchard is allowed to send
notifications in the phone's own settings. For email, check your spam folder and add
hello@orchard.example to your contacts. If you still receive nothing, send a note to the
team with the kind of notification you expected and roughly when.
