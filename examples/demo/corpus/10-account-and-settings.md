# Account and settings

**Changing your name or photo.** Open **Settings**, tap your name at the top, and edit.
Your name is what other people in your Family see on your albums and comments.

**Changing your email or password.** Open **Settings**, then **Account**. Changing your
email sends a confirmation to the new address; the change happens when you open the link.

**Two-step sign-in.** Under **Account**, turn on **Two-step sign-in** to be asked for a
code from an authenticator app when signing in on a new device.

**Storage.** **Settings** shows how much of your Family's storage is used. Every Family
starts with 100 GB. The Organizer can see who is using how much under **Sharing**. If you
run out, delete photos you no longer want (they leave storage after 30 days in
**Recently deleted**) or ask the Organizer about more space.

**Downloading your photos.** Under **Account**, choose **Download everything**. Orchard
prepares a set of files with your originals and the albums they belong to, and emails you
a link when it is ready, usually within an hour. The link works for 3 days.

**Deleting your account.** Under **Account**, choose **Delete account**. If you are the
Organizer of a Family, make someone else Organizer first. Your own albums and the photos
you added are deleted after 30 days; photos you added to other people's albums stay in
those albums. This cannot be undone once the 30 days have passed.

**Languages.** Orchard is available in English, Spanish, and Swedish. Change it under
**Settings**, **Language**. Album names and comments are shown as they were written.

**Getting help.** The **?** button on every screen opens the help panel. It answers from
this guide, and it can send your question or idea to the Orchard team.
