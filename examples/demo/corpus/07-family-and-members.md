# Family and members

**Inviting someone.** Only the Organizer can invite. Open **Sharing**, choose
**Invite to Family**, and enter their email address. They get an email with a link that
works for 7 days. When they accept, they become a Member and appear in the **Sharing**
list. If the invitation lapses, send it again from the same place.

**Who is in the Family.** **Sharing** lists every Member, with a star next to the
Organizer. Tap a name to see the albums they own and the albums shared with them.

**Removing someone.** The Organizer opens **Sharing**, taps the person's name, and
chooses **Remove from Family**. They lose access to every shared album at once. Photos
they added to other people's albums stay in those albums. Albums they own leave the
Family with them.

**Leaving a Family.** Any Member can leave from **Settings** with **Leave Family**. Your
own albums come with you and are no longer visible to the Family. Photos you added to
other people's albums stay there.

**Changing the Organizer.** The current Organizer opens **Sharing**, taps a Member, and
chooses **Make Organizer**. There is one Organizer at a time.

**Being in two Families.** One account can be in several Families, for example your own
and your partner's parents'. Switch between them from the Family name at the top of the
screen. Albums, people, and settings are separate in each Family.

**Children's accounts.** Orchard does not offer accounts for children under 13. Photos of
children are added and managed by the adults in the Family.
