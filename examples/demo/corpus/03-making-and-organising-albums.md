# Making and organising albums

An album is a named collection of photos. A photo can live in as many albums as you like
without being copied.

**Making an album.** On **Albums**, choose **New album**, type a name, and press Enter.
The album is empty until you add photos or move some in.

**Renaming or describing an album.** Open the album, choose the **⋯** menu, then
**Edit album**. You can change the name and add a short description that shows at the
top of the album for everyone it is shared with.

**Putting a photo in another album.** Open the photo, choose **Add to album**, and pick
the album. The photo now appears in both. Removing it from one album does not remove it
from the other.

**Choosing the cover.** Open the photo you want as the cover and choose **Set as cover**
from its **⋯** menu.

**Ordering.** Albums show newest photos first. To see oldest first, open the album,
choose the **⋯** menu, and switch **Order** to **Oldest first**. Orchard remembers your
choice per album. There is no way to drag photos into a custom order.

**Removing a photo from an album.** Open the photo and choose **Remove from album**. The
photo stays in your library and in any other albums.

**Deleting a photo for good.** Open the photo and choose **Delete**. It moves to
**Recently deleted** under **Settings**, where it stays for 30 days before it is gone
for good. You can put it back from there. Only the person who added a photo, or the
Organizer, can delete it.

**Deleting an album.** Open the album, choose the **⋯** menu, then **Delete album**. The
photos in it are not deleted; they stay in your library and in other albums.
