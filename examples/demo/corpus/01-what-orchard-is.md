# What Orchard is

Orchard is a private photo home for your family. You add photos, sort them into albums,
tag the people in them, and share albums with the relatives you choose. Nothing in
Orchard is public. Nobody outside your family can find your photos, and nobody inside it
sees an album unless it was shared with them.

Your family is called a **Family** in Orchard. One person creates the Family and becomes
its **Organizer**; everyone else joins by invitation and is a **Member**. Both can add
photos and make albums. Only the Organizer can invite and remove people, change the
Family name, or delete the Family.

The main screens are **Albums**, **People**, **Sharing**, and **Settings**. You reach them
from the bar at the top on a computer or the tabs at the bottom on a phone.

If the guide does not cover something you need, use **Send this to the team** in the help
panel. A person reads every message.
